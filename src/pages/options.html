BetterWebWork

Installed
  1. Open WeBWorK as usual. Any page with /webwork2/ in its address is restyled.
  2. The gear at the top right of every WeBWorK page opens these settings, right on the page.
  3. Pick a theme for the day and one for night below, or import one you made for Collegiapp or PocketCanvas.
  4. Nothing leaves your browser. Settings and your attempt log stay in the add-on's storage, and answers you type stay in the WeBWorK site's own storage until you submit them.
