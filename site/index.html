Problem 1 1 point

What changes

Nothing about how WeBWorK grades. Every form is sent exactly as WeBWorK wrote it; only the page around it changes.

  • One problem, centered

    One bar on top with the course, set and problem as breadcrumbs. Wide equations widen the sheet instead of scrolling it.

  • The whole set, one click away

    Each problem is marked correct, partly correct, wrong or not started. [ and ] move between them.

  • Notes on wrong answers

    What you typed, how WeBWorK read it, and a note on common slips like e^-2x or sin x. Quiet when there's nothing certain to say.

  • Your typing is kept

    A refresh or a trip to another problem doesn't lose what you typed in the blanks. Undo is one click.

  • Due dates in words

    "Thu 11:59 PM, in 4 days", the reduced-credit window, and your progress in points.

  • Dark mode and themes

    Paper by day, Paper Dark at night, or a Pocket theme. Every color can be adjusted, with contrast checks.

  • A second press on your last try

    On your last attempt, Submit asks to be pressed again. Typing an answer that was already marked wrong shows when you tried it.

  • Nothing leaves your browser

    No account, no server, no analytics. It only runs on pages whose address has /webwork2/ in it.

Want to see what it restyles? Turn the switch in the top bar off: this page redraws the way WeBWorK draws its own.

    Install

    One extension for Firefox 128+, Chrome 111+ and Edge. Once it's on, open any WeBWorK problem; there's nothing to set up.

    The store listings are in review. Until they're up, load it from the source in a minute:

    Firefox You're using this

    Get it for Firefox

    From Firefox Add-ons, for Firefox 128 or later.

    1. Download the code from GitHub and unzip it.
    2. Open about:debugging#/runtime/this-firefox.
    3. Click Load Temporary Add-on and pick manifest.json. It stays until Firefox restarts.

    Chrome and Edge You're using this

    Get it for Chrome

    From the Chrome Web Store, for Chrome 111 or later and Edge.

    1. Download the code from GitHub and unzip it.
    2. Open chrome://extensions (or edge://extensions) and turn on Developer mode.
    3. Click Load unpacked and pick the folder.

    Problem 5 1 point

    Questions about WeBWorK

    Why does WeBWorK mark my answer incorrect when I think it's right?

    Usually because WeBWorK read what you typed differently from how you meant it. The common ones:

    • e^-2x is read as (e^-2)·x. Write e^(-2x).
    • x^1/2 is read as (x^1)/2. Write x^(1/2) or sqrt(x).
    • sin x without parentheses can be read as sin of only part of what follows. Write sin(x).
    • 1/2x is ambiguous. Write 1/(2x) or (1/2)x.
    • A decimal rounded too early, like 0.33 for 1/3. WeBWorK usually wants about 0.1% accuracy, so give 4 or more significant digits or the exact value.

    Press Preview My Answers first: it shows how WeBWorK read your answer and doesn't use an attempt. The full list is in How to type answers in WeBWorK.

    How do I type square roots, exponents, pi and infinity in WeBWorK?

    sqrt(x) for a square root, ^ for a power (x^2, e^(3x)), pi for π, inf for infinity, * for times, and abs(x) for an absolute value. ln(x) and log(x) are both the natural log; base 10 is log10(x). See the cheat sheet for intervals, points and vectors.

    Does WeBWorK have a dark mode?

    WeBWorK's own theme is light only. BetterWebWork adds Paper Dark, which turns on with your system's dark mode, on a schedule, or always. Graphs get a light panel behind them so their lines stay visible.

    What's the difference between Preview, Check and Submit?

    Preview My Answers shows how WeBWorK read what you typed and doesn't count as an attempt. Check Answers, when your instructor allows it, tells you which answers are right without recording a score. Submit Answers records your score and uses an attempt. With BetterWebWork, Ctrl+Enter previews and Ctrl+Shift+Enter submits.

    Will I lose my answers if I refresh a WeBWorK page?

    WeBWorK keeps the answers you submit, but answers you typed and haven't sent yet can be lost on a refresh or when you open another problem. BetterWebWork saves the blanks as you type and puts them back, with a note and Undo.

    What does "Can't generate enough valid points for comparison" mean?

    WeBWorK checks a formula by plugging numbers into yours and the correct one. This message means yours couldn't be worked out at enough of them, often a square root or log of a negative number, or a division by zero. Check the domain of each piece. More messages are explained in WeBWorK's error messages.

    Does BetterWebWork change my grade or give answers?

    No. It never changes what's sent to WeBWorK, and its notes only say what's certain from what you typed and from WeBWorK's message. It never guesses at the right answer, which the page doesn't have until WeBWorK shows it.

    Does it work with my school's WeBWorK?

    It runs on any address with /webwork2/ in it. It's built against WeBWorK 2.18 and also reads 2.19's feedback. If a page looks wrong, the switch in the toolbar turns it off for that site, and you can report it on GitHub.

    What does it store, and where?

    Your settings, themes and attempt log stay in your browser's extension storage. Answers you haven't submitted yet stay in the WeBWorK site's own storage in your browser. Nothing is sent anywhere. The full privacy policy is short.
